GET FUNDED! — RANKS, TITLES AND BADGES
Completed in the open Studio place. The .rbxl was not saved and the game was not published.

CHANGES
- Eight permanent progression ranks, separate from numerical leaderboard placement.
- Sixteen achievement titles earned from the existing authoritative closed-trade counters, profitable closes and lifetime net trading profit.
- Rank gates combine all three metrics. Losses and restarts do not revoke earned ranks or titles. Award IDs prevent duplicates.
- Existing award IDs and historical award evidence are retained. Old title strings are archived in LegacyProgression; obsolete account-tier names become the permanent Founding Trader alumni title. Existing balances, histories and protection preferences are retained.
- New titles can recognize saved authoritative profit high-water evidence during migration. New rank gates use that historical evidence alongside monotonically accumulated trade/win counts.
- Custom ImageGen artwork was generated as a transparent 16-emblem atlas and uploaded to Roblox: rbxassetid://114491192679289. The local original is icons/rank-badges/trader-badges-atlas.png. Roblox's imported texture is 1024x1024; measured transparent gutters define the crop rectangles.
- Progress features the current rank, featured title, next title, readable progress bars, title collection and rank ladder. Earned/next/locked states have text and symbols. Badge hover and a short unlock highlight provide restrained feedback.
- Both leaderboards use server-projected rank IDs and the shared definitions/artwork. Legacy snapshots show Rank updating instead of guessing. Placement medals identify first, second and third; the local row includes YOU. Sorting, cents, tie ordering and persistence format are unchanged.
- Long player names wrap; tapping a row opens full details. Very large scores abbreviate only when necessary and remain exact in the details panel.
- Progress and Leaderboard use the trading UI's Fredoka font. No automatic text scaling is used on those pages. Responsive layouts cover portrait and short landscape views.
- Player-facing run numbers and obsolete tier titles are removed. Stats no longer constructs the attempt-count and closed-trade summary labels/icons. Statistics and trade history remain intact.
